---
description: The Monday operations review, written from three commands. The week, what needs a decision, the compliance state, then the five things that matter this week.
---

Run these three, in this order, and write the review from what they return. Do not write anything they do not support.

```
npm run ops -- week
npm run ops -- attention
npm run ops -- compliance
```

Then write it in this shape, no more than a page:

1. **The week in one line.** Shifts, handovers on time, trips, injuries, rounds missed, readings outside limits.
2. **The one that cannot wait.** A notifiable event not notified, a safety-critical bypass past its authority, a safe operating limit excursion nobody has logged. By name. Empty is the system working.
3. **Handovers.** Missing, not accepted, late, and any bypass left off a handover.
4. **The plant.** Trips, excursions and the tags that keep appearing; bypasses still on and for how long.
5. **People.** Lapsed sign-offs and anyone who worked without one; fatigue limits passed; standing orders not read.
6. **Paperwork.** Standing orders expiring this week, sign-offs due in 30 days, overdue actions.
7. **The five things to do this week.** From the attention list, weighted people's safety first, the law second, the plant third. Say why each made the list.
8. **One thing to decide.** The single item that needs a manager, not a process.
